Privacy Policy for PawnStorm

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Summary

PawnStorm does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network requests. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Information We Collect

None.

PawnStorm does not collect personal information of any kind. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying detail. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user account.

Information Stored on Your Device

PawnStorm saves a small amount of data locally on your device so the app can remember your progress between sessions:

  • Which opening lines you have completed
  • Your daily practice streak
  • Your preferred practice mode
  • Opening lines you have starred
  • Chess lines you have imported or saved yourself

This information is stored only on your device, using the operating system's standard local storage. It is never transmitted anywhere, and we have no access to it. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

Network Access

PawnStorm does not connect to the internet. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising networks, and no third-party software development kits. No data leaves your device at any time.

Third-Party Services

PawnStorm uses no third-party services.

Children's Privacy

PawnStorm is safe for users of all ages. Because the app collects no information whatsoever, it collects nothing from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Your Rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete on request. All data created by the app lives on your device and is fully under your control. You can remove it at any time by deleting the app.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of PawnStorm adds features that involve collecting or transmitting data, this policy will be updated before those features ship, and the effective date above will change.
