Reporting a wrong move

If PawnStorm marks a move wrong that you believe is correct, that's the most useful thing you can report. Please include:

  • The opening and variation name
  • The moves played up to that point
  • The move you played, and the move the app expected

A screenshot of the board covers most of this on its own.

Common questions

Does my progress sync between devices?
No. Everything is stored locally on your device. There's no account and no server, so progress doesn't transfer.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Since nothing is stored off-device, deleting the app removes your completed lines, streak, and saved lines permanently.

Can I add my own openings?
Yes. Use Import PGN from the main menu. Paste any PGN and it will be saved to My Lines, where you can practice it like any built-in opening.

Does the app work offline?
Always. PawnStorm never connects to the internet.

Is there a subscription?
No. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads.
